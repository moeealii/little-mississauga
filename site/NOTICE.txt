MODIFICATIONS AND ART PROVENANCE
The earlier gallery contains historical source, teacher and model comparisons; they are not adjoining city coverage and are not imported as the continuous map. ARTIFACTS AND LIMITS
Illustrative model output is not certified geography, a survey, a seamless 3D reconstruction, a navigational or trail-safety map, live traffic, or current venue occupancy. All current City Hall AI variants are withheld pending the clock-tower and iconic-architecture source/teacher/model audit, not just strength 0.8. Generated buildings, roofs, water edges and small objects can be wrong. A diagnostic result or a file hash is not architectural acceptance. No unrelated historical teacher plates are smeared into artificial full-city coverage.
